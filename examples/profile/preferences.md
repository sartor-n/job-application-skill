# Narrative Preferences

Fictional example for Sam Okafor.

## Lead Differentiator
Shipped a regulated clinical data product to production in three NHS trusts, not a pilot. Open every summary with production delivery in a regulated setting.

## Experience Claims
* Total professional experience: 12 years.
* Product management since 2019. Never attach the 12 years to product management specifically.

## Title Rules
* Larkspur Systems: always "Senior Product Manager", never "Lead" or "Principal".

## Naming Rules
* Use "Tidewater Insights", never the internal codename "Project Delta".
* "Data platform" is two words, never hyphenated.

## Themes to Promote in Every Archetype
* Regulated-environment delivery.
* Platform work used by other squads.
* Measurable clinician adoption.

## Framing Rules (say this, not that)
* Clinical safety: say "contributed to and drove the safety case reviews", not "owned clinical governance".
* Customer work: say "co-designed with trusts", not "managed the vendor relationship".

## Fabrication Traps
* `12 years of product`: false, product roles date from 2019.
* `led a team of 20`: false, the team was 4.
* `national rollout`: false, three trusts.

## Banned Phrases
* `results-driven`
* `passionate about`
* `synergy`
* `leveraged`

## Bullet Style
* Every experience bullet covers one concept, at most two lines, no filler.
* Lead achievements with the product or thing name, then the result with a number.
* Enforce hardest on the current role.

## Summary Format
* Three short paragraphs separated by blank lines, first person, no "I am a".
* Bold the top three to four job description keywords in the summary.

## Target Title Placement
* Use the target job title in the header and the cover letter only. Work history titles stay factual.

## Contact Rules
* Show the work-rights line only in NL applications.
* Never print the phone number.

## Location and Relocation Facts
* Based in Leeds. For NL roles, state "relocating to Amsterdam, available Q2 2027". Never imply already resident in the Netherlands.

## Early Career Handling
* Keep the analyst role at Tidewater Freight only when space allows; compress it to two bullets in two-page markets.
