## Raw JD
Harbourline Health is looking for a Senior Product Manager to own our new data products line. You will define the roadmap for bed-management dashboards and forecasting, work day to day with our data engineering and data science teams, and partner with clinical and operational leaders at our hospital customers.

What you will do: own the data products roadmap; define adoption and outcome metrics; run discovery with hospital operations teams; write clear specifications for pipelines and models; work with our clinical safety and information governance leads.

What you bring: 5+ years in product management with 2+ in data or analytics; experience shipping into hospitals or another regulated environment; comfort with SQL and data models; strong communication with clinical and operational stakeholders. Nice to have: NHS procurement experience, forecasting features, mentoring.

Hybrid, Manchester, two days a week on site. Salary band 75k to 90k GBP plus equity.
