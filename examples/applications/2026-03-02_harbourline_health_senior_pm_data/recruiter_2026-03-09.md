## 1. Opening pitch (60 to 90 seconds, rehearse it)
Product manager, 12 years in data-heavy regulated businesses, the last four on data products. At Larkspur I took Tidewater Insights, a clinical reporting product, from discovery to production in three NHS trusts; 1,200 clinicians use it every week and report turnaround went from six days to same day. Before that I shipped a forecasting feature for a logistics operator that lifted fleet utilisation by 11%. Harbourline is standing up a data products line for bed management, which is the same shape of problem in a domain I already work in, so the timing is right.

* Harbourline is moving from workflow software into data products; I have done that transition once already.
* Bed management is where ward managers, my current users, feel the most pain.
* A 40-hospital customer base is a step up in scale from three trusts.
