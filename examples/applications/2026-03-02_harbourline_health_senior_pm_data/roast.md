## 1. Recommended Archetype and Fit Score

* **Archetype**: `[IC]` (hands-on data PM; the JD wants someone who writes specs for pipelines and reads data models, with mentoring only as a nice-to-have)
* **Fit Score**: 84 / 100

Harbourline is hiring someone to build a data products line from scratch inside a hospital software company. Sam has done almost exactly this at Larkspur: a regulated clinical reporting product taken from discovery to 1,200 weekly active clinicians across three trusts. The forecasting nice-to-have is covered by the Tidewater Freight work. The main risk is domain adjacency: patient flow and bed management, not clinical reporting.

## 3. Gaps and Mitigations

1. **Domain: patient flow and bed management**
   * *Gap*: Sam's clinical work is reporting for ward managers, not patient-flow operations.
   * *Mitigation*: Ward managers are the bed-management users. Frame the Insights work around ward-level operational data; mention Open Ward Metrics.
2. **Team size and company stage**
   * *Gap*: Harbourline is 250 people with 6 PMs; Larkspur is smaller.
   * *Mitigation*: Emphasise platform work used by several squads and the NHS procurement experience (a nice-to-have they listed).
3. **Location**
   * *Gap*: Manchester, two days on site; Sam is in Leeds.
   * *Mitigation*: Commutable. State Leeds plainly, no relocation language needed for the UK market.
