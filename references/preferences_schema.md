# preferences.md Schema

What each heading means and what a good entry looks like, using a fictional product manager, Sam Okafor, as the example.

## Lead Differentiator
The one thing few candidates can truthfully claim. It opens the CV summary and anchors the pitch.
> Shipped a regulated clinical data product to production in three NHS trusts, not a pilot. Open every summary with production delivery in a regulated setting.

## Experience Claims
Total years, discipline years, and the phrasing that would be a lie.
> 12 years professional experience, product management since 2019. "12 years in product" is a fabrication. "12 years turning data into clinical and commercial outcomes" is fine.

## Title Rules
Roles whose title must never be upscaled.
> Larkspur Systems: always "Senior Product Manager", never "Lead" or "Principal", even for senior applications. Target titles go in the header and cover letter only.

## Naming Rules
Public names over internal ones, spelling rules.
> Use "Tidewater Insights", never the internal codename "Project Delta". "Data platform" is two words, never hyphenated.

## Themes to Promote in Every Archetype
Two or three threads that appear in every version, dialled up or down.
> Regulated-environment delivery; cross-team platform work used by other squads; measurable clinician adoption.

## Framing Rules (say this, not that)
Preferred and avoided framings for sensitive topics.
> Clinical safety: say "contributed to and drove the safety case reviews", not "owned clinical governance". Customer work: say "co-designed with trusts", not "managed the vendor relationship".

## Fabrication Traps
Plausible-sounding claims that are false. Exact phrases in backticks; the linter flags them.
> `12 years of product`: false, product roles date from 2019.
> `led a team of 20`: false, the squad was 7.

## Banned Phrases
> `results-driven`, `passionate about`, `synergy`, `leveraged`.

## Bullet Style
Defaults offered at onboarding:
> One concept per bullet, at most two lines, no filler. Lead achievements with the thing, then the number. Enforce hardest on the current role.

## Summary Format
> Three short paragraphs, first person, no "I am a". Bold the top three or four JD keywords.

## Target Title Placement
> The target job title appears in the header and the cover letter only. Work-history titles stay factual.

## Contact Rules
Anything beyond `profile.yaml`.
> Show the work-rights line only in UK and NL markets. Never print the phone number.

## Location and Relocation Facts
> Based in Leeds. Applying to NL roles as "relocating to Amsterdam, available from Q2 2027". Never imply already resident in the Netherlands.

## Early Career Handling
> Keep the Tidewater Freight analyst role only in three-page markets. Drop it for two-page markets.
